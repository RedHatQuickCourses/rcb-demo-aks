3.2. Deploying RHOSO on the RHOCP Cluster

After preparing the RHOCP cluster and namespaced resources, the next step is to deploy RHOSO on the RHOCP cluster. This process involves several key activities:

3.2.1. Prerequisites

  • Ensure that the RHOSO environment is deployed on a Red Hat OpenShift Container Platform (RHOCP) cluster. For detailed instructions, refer to the Deploying Red Hat OpenStack Services on OpenShift guide.

  • Log in to a workstation with access to the RHOCP cluster as a user with cluster-admin privileges.

3.2.2. Deploying RHOSO

To deploy RHOSO on the RHOCP cluster, follow these steps:

Step 1: Access the RHOCP Console

  • Open your web browser and navigate to the RHOCP console. The URL typically follows this pattern: https://<cluster_name>-<domain_name>.apps.example.com. Replace <cluster_name> and <domain_name> with your actual cluster name and domain.

Step 2: Create a New Project

  • Once logged in, create a new project for RHOSO deployment. This helps in organizing resources and maintaining a clear separation between different environments. To create a new project, click on "Projects" in the left-hand menu, then click "Create Project." Enter a name for your project (e.g., rhoso-project) and click "Create."

Step 3: Deploy RHOSO Operator

  • In the RHOCP console, navigate to "Operators" > "OperatorHub." Search for "Red Hat OpenStack Services on OpenShift" and click on the operator card. Click "Install" and follow the prompts to install the RHOSO Operator in your newly created project.

Step 4: Create RHOSO Custom Resource

  • After the RHOSO Operator is installed, navigate to "Operators" > "Installed Operators" and click on the "Red Hat OpenStack Services on OpenShift" operator.
